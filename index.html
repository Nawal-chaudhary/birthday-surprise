A Letter For You

My Alina,

I don't think you fully realise what happened to the world the day you were born. It got quieter, in the best way. Softer. Warmer. Like someone turned the lights down low and made everything feel like home.

And somehow — out of all the people, all the timing, all the tiny decisions that had to line up perfectly — I got to be the one who knows you. The one who gets your good mornings. The one who sees you laugh at your own jokes and cry at movies you've already seen ten times.

Thank you for being gentle with me. For being patient. For choosing me on the ordinary days, not just the beautiful ones. You have this quiet way of making everything feel survivable, and I hope you know what a rare and precious thing that is.

Today isn't just your birthday. It's the anniversary of the world getting its very best thing. And I get to celebrate it beside you — which feels like the luckiest thing that has ever happened to me.

Happy birthday, my favourite person. Here's to a year that loves you back exactly as much as you deserve. 🌻

always, yours 💛
